ShiftChange Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the ShiftChange website and the ShiftChange mobile app

ShiftChange is a crew rostering service operated by Bodi James Walden, ABN 68 998 089 653 ("we", "us"). Organisations (your employer) use ShiftChange to plan and publish work rosters. This policy explains what personal information ShiftChange handles, why, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Name and email addressYour employer, when they set up your accountTo sign you in and show you your roster
Login identifierCreated when your account is madeTo link your login to your roster
Work roster details: shifts, hours, flight bookings, rig, crew, role, swing patternYour employer's office staffTo show you your roster

The mobile app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. It contains no advertising or third-party tracking. It does not send push notifications.

How it is used and shared

Your information is used only to provide ShiftChange to you and your employer. Your roster is visible to you and to your employer's authorised office staff. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

We use these service providers to run ShiftChange:

  • Auth0 (Okta): secure sign-in. Australian region.
  • Supabase: database hosting. Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2).
  • Vercel: hosting for the ShiftChange website.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On your phone, your sign-in is stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Your password is entered on Auth0's sign-in page and is never seen or stored by the ShiftChange app. Database access rules mean each staff member can read only their own roster.

How long we keep it

Roster records are kept for as long as your employer uses ShiftChange, or until your employer removes them. When your account is deleted, your login is removed; your employer may keep its own roster records as required for its business and legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Access and correction: ask your employer's office, or contact us.
  • Delete your account: see Delete your ShiftChange account, or use Delete my account in the app's account menu.
  • Complaints: contact us first. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
