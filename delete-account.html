Delete your ShiftChange account

You can ask for your ShiftChange login to be deleted at any time.

How to ask

Email us from the address you sign in with, so we can confirm it's you:

Email a deletion request

Or write to support@shiftchange.com.au with the subject "Delete my ShiftChange account".

What happens

  • We confirm the request with you and delete your login within 30 days.
  • Your name, email and login are removed from our sign-in service, and you can no longer sign in to the app or website.
  • Roster records your employer created are your employer's work records. They may keep them for their business and legal obligations; ask your employer if you want those removed too.

See also our privacy policy.
